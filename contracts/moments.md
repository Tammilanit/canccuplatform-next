# Moments contract

Moments is the venue's autopilot at the peaks: one server-side arbiter ranks, times, queues and refuses everything that claims a screen, per zone, and Screen places what it decides ([screen.md](screen.md#moments-drive-it-claims), step 4 plan D1). The `moments` module owns it. Nothing else emits screen overrides ([events.md](events.md#screen-overrides)).

**Machine-readable API: [moments.openapi.yaml](moments.openapi.yaml)** (OpenAPI 3.1). It is the source for paths, fields and status codes; this file explains them and adds the rules and events. Conventions (field names, times, error body): [README.md](README.md#conventions).

The contract comes in parts, each with the package whose code uses it (step 4 plan D24): **the engine** (moments, priorities, the arbiter's rules, the crew spotlight, emergency, built-in rules, history), **scenes** (below), **cues and sound** (#113, below: [Cues and sound](#cues-and-sound)), **announcements** (#115, below: [Announcements](#announcements-115)) and **storyboards** (#114, below: [Storyboards](#storyboards-114)).

## Tenancy
- Moments belong to one event, which is one tenant ([0013](../docs/decisions/0013-multi-tenant-core.md)); the tenant comes from the request's hostname. Every row carries `tenant_id` under row-level security. Events go to `t:<tenant>:events:moments`.
- An archived event's Moments are read-only: writes get 409 `event_archived`. Archiving ends every moment, the emergency included (E10, below).
- The one action across events is the superadmin's venue-wide emergency (Emergency, below).

## Words
- A **moment** is one thing that wants screen time: a crew spotlight, an emergency, a rule's hot game. It is a row in Postgres from its request to its end; a restart of Moments loses nothing (E2).
- Moments shows a moment by emitting one or more **overrides** ([events.md](events.md#screen-overrides)); Screen turns them into claims and places them by `rank` and the region chain (`regions`).
- Each moment has a **class**, and the event's **priority order** of the classes gives its `rank`.
- **Patience** is how long a moment waits for its first place on a zone before it gives that zone up.

## Classes and the priority order
| Class | Default rank | Kind, regions | Who starts it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `emergency` | 100, locked at the top | `takeover`, `["full"]` | people with `moments.emergency`, the superadmin |
| `stream` | 90 | (the Cast track, later) | — |
| `announcement` | 80 | `block`, `["rail", "ticker"]` (an announcement's chain follows its level, below) | the announcement composer (#115), the pizza rule, "All clear" |
| `spotlight` | 70 | `block`, `["hero"]` | the crew (`moments.take`) |
| `storyboard` | 65 | `block`, `["hero", "rail"]` (`["full"]` for a full-screen story, a video step) | stories (Storyboards, below) |
| `tournament` | 60 | `block`, `["hero", "rail"]` | rules |
| `finals` | 55 | `block`, `["hero"]` | rules |
| `hot_game` | 50 | `block`, `["hero", "rail"]` | rules |
| `reactions` | 20 | (the App track, later) | — |
| `ticker` | 10 | `block`, `["ticker"]` | modules |

- **The order:** the event has one order of the ten classes, highest first; a zone may keep its own. A class's rank is the rank of its place: the n-th class in an order gets the n-th of 100, 90, 80, 70, 65, 60, 55, 50, 20, 10. The default order is the table's. `emergency` is always first, in every order, so nothing outranks it.
- `GET /api/moments/priorities` (right `moments.read`) → `PriorityOrders`: `order` (the event's), `zones` (each `zone_key` and its own `order`; a zone that follows the event is not listed), `ranks` (each class's rank in the event's order), `revision`.
- `PUT /api/moments/priorities` (right `moments.settings.write`) with `order`, `zones` and `revision` replaces both. Each order names every class once, `emergency` first (else 400 `invalid_request`); a stale `revision` is 409 `version_conflict`. Audited.
- **A priority change takes effect at once** (E8): every running moment is ranked again and its overrides get the new `rank` through `.changed`. A moment that loses its region is hidden or waits; a priority change never ends one.
- **Zones with their own order:** an override has one `rank`, so a moment whose zones rank it differently is shown by more than one override. The override for the zones that follow the event's order has the moment's id; each zone whose own order ranks it differently gets an override of its own, `<moment id>.<zone key>`, and keeps it for the moment's life (two such zones with the same rank still get one each). Override ids are opaque to Screen. The zones a moment's overrides cover never overlap.

## Moments
A `Moment`: `id`, `class`, `rank` (in the event's order), `kind`, `regions`, `template`, `content`, `zones` (empty: every zone of the event), `origin` (`rule`, `crew`, `emergency` or `module`), `rule_id` (or null), `source` (as the Override's), `actor` (`user_id` and `name` of the person, or null), `starts_at`, `expires_at` (or null: no end of its own), `patience_seconds`, `qr` and `binding` (Live and interactive, below), `placements` (each zone Screen reported: `zone_key`, `state`, `role` or null, `since`), `ended_at`, `end_reason`.

- `GET /api/moments/moments` (right `moments.read`) → the running moments, highest rank first, with the zones Moments knows (`zones`: each zone key Screen has named, from its zone events). The On air page reads it.
- **A request** (`MomentRequest`): `class`, `template`, `content`, and optionally `zones`, `regions` (default the class's), `ttl_seconds`, `patience_seconds`, `qr`, `binding`, `source`, `dedupe_key`. Modules send one to `POST /internal/moments/moments?tenant_id=` (the kit's service identity); people use the crew actions below. The `emergency` class is never requested this way (400 `invalid_request`).
- **Answers:** 201 with the `Moment` when it started, 200 with it when it renewed one (E4, below), 409 `moment_refused` with `retry_after_seconds` (30) and `held_by` (the moment that holds its regions) when it was refused.

### The arbiter's rules
The legacy directive rules, moved to the server and made regional:
- **TTL:** a moment lives `ttl_seconds` (default 120, 1 to 7200); `expires_at` is its start plus that. Screen ends the claim at `expires_at` by itself, also while Moments is down. An emergency has no TTL.
- **Re-posting extends it** (E4): a request whose `dedupe_key` (default: the rule and the source's `ref`) names a running moment renews it: with the same `template` and `content` only `expires_at` moves (to now plus its TTL, the request's or else the moment's own, but never earlier than it was, so a crew's Extend holds; `.changed` with `changed: ["expires_at"]`); with new content the content changes too. `starts_at` never moves.
- **Refusal with retry:** a rule's moment (and a module's) is refused when, on every zone it names, every role of its chain is held by a higher rank, as Screen last reported (a `full` claim holds every role but `overlay`). The answer, and the rule, wait 30 s before asking again. People's moments are never refused: they wait.
- **Share before you queue:** an accepted moment takes the first free role of its chain on each zone (Screen's placement). Where none is free it waits, at most `patience_seconds` (default: 120 for a rule's, its whole TTL for people's), then gives that zone up (`.ended` there, `preempted`). Patience counts only until its first place on the zone: once shown, a moment that is pushed aside waits hidden until its own end.
- **Revert:** when a higher moment ends, Screen places the ones it held aside again where they would be now, and the history notes them as back. A moment whose time ran out meanwhile does not come back.
- **Ends:** by its `expires_at` (`expired`), by a person (`cleared`), by its patience (`preempted`), or when its source ends (`source_ended`: a surge ended, a match started, the event was archived, its zone was removed).

### Placement from Screen
Moments follows `screen.claims.changed` ([screen.md](screen.md#events-version-1)): for each zone, which of its overrides are `placed` (and in which role), `unplaced` (waiting) or `hidden` (under a `full` claim). It stores each as the moment's placement on that zone and writes the history from it. `screen.zone.saved` and `screen.zone.removed` tell it the event's zones; a moment leaves a removed zone (`source_ended`, E9).

### The overrides it emits
`moments.override.started`, `.changed` and `.ended` ([events.md](events.md#screen-overrides)) with the fields of [screen.md](screen.md#moments-drive-it-claims). `ttl_seconds` is never sent: `expires_at` always is (null for an emergency). `.ended` carries the additive field `final` (below). An override whose moment's cue has a picture carries `effect` ([Cues and sound](#the-picture)).

- **Narrowing (D3):** when an override leaves some of its zones but stays on others, Moments first emits `.changed` with `zones` narrowed to the explicit list it still holds (`changed` names `zones`), then `.ended` with just the zones it left and `final: false`. An override that held every zone (`zones: []`) is narrowed to the zones Moments knows minus the ones it leaves.
- **`final`** (boolean, additive): `true` only when the override is gone everywhere. An `.ended` with `zones: []` ends it everywhere and is always `final: true`.

### Live and interactive (D18)
- **`qr`** (`path` on the event's hostname, starting with `/`, and `label`): the moment's view-model gets `qr` (`url`, `label`, `image_url`) as Screen's widgets do ([screen.md](screen.md#qr-handoff)), so the template draws the code.
- **`binding`** (`source`: `leaderboard`, `session` with `ref` the session id, or `none`): the content follows that source. Moments keeps the latest tournament leaderboard and sessions from the catalog's events, and each change re-renders the bound moments' content and sends it in `.changed` (`changed: ["content"]`). Before the source has spoken the content is a placeholder ("Collecting standings…").

## The crew spotlight (D18, L15)
`POST /api/moments/spotlight` (right `moments.take`) with `show` (`standings`, `bracket`, `photo` or `message`), `zones` (default every zone), `duration_seconds` (default 300, 1 to 7200), and per `show`: `session_id` (bracket), `photo_url` and `caption` (a path on the event's hostname, photo), `title` and `body` (message); optionally `qr`. It starts a `spotlight` moment in the hero: standings as `leaderboard` and the bracket as `bracket` (both bound, live), a photo as `photo`, a message as `announcement` (level `info`). The content carries `from_crew: true`, so the screen says "From the crew" and never the name; the history keeps who started it.

## Emergency (mockup 07, U4, E10)
- `POST /api/moments/emergency` (right `moments.emergency`) with optional `title`, `body` (defaults: "Please leave the hall", "Follow the exit signs.") and `zones` (default every zone): a takeover in `full` at rank 100 with template `announcement` and level `emergency`, no end time, `kind` `takeover`. One per event: a second start while one runs is 409 `emergency_active`. The page asks for a 2-second hold before it sends.
- `POST /api/moments/emergency/end` (right `moments.emergency`) with `all_clear` (boolean): one tap ends it everywhere (`cleared`); with `all_clear` an "All clear" `announcement` takes the rail for 60 s. Every moment it held aside comes back if its time has not run out (Revert).
- **Venue-wide:** the superadmin, on the superadmin hostname only, `POST /api/moments/venue-emergency` with `venue_id`, `title` and `body`, and `POST /api/moments/venue-emergency/end` with `venue_id` and `all_clear`, starts or ends the emergency of every event at that venue that is not archived (404 `not_found` without one). Each event's history names the superadmin.
- Nothing outranks it, and no rule, module or scene starts or ends one: only these four paths do. Archiving the event ends it with every other moment (`source_ended`).

## Crew actions on running moments (U12)
- `POST /api/moments/moments/{id}/end` (right `moments.take`) with optional `zone_key`: ends a crew moment everywhere, or, with `zone_key`, any moment on that zone only ("End in zone"; a rule's moment ends there for this occurrence: the rule fires again next time). An emergency needs `moments.emergency` and ends only through Emergency.
- `POST /api/moments/moments/{id}/extend` (right `moments.take`) with `seconds` (1 to 3600): moves `expires_at` on, at most 7200 s after now.

## Idempotency (E18)
Every crew action (`POST` on the spotlight, emergency, venue emergency, end and extend) carries a key, the header `Idempotency-Key` (8 to 64 characters of `A–Z a–z 0–9 - _`; without one 400 `invalid_request`). The same key from the same person within 24 hours answers what the first request answered and does nothing again, so a phone's retry never sends twice. The pages put a fresh key in each form.

## Built-in rules (D7, D8)
Rules turn catalog events into moments. Each built-in has these settings per event: `enabled`, `template` (one of the rule's), `class` (its rank), `regions`, `ttl_seconds`, `zones` and `cue` (the cue its moment's start plays, or null: [Cues and sound](#cues-and-sound)). The defaults: `match_called` `tournament.start`, `standings` none (a leaderboard changes too often to sound), `finals_result` `tournament.post`, `pizza` `pizza.placed`, `hot_game` `chrome.hot`. `GET /api/moments/rules` (right `moments.read`) lists them with their `revision`; `PUT /api/moments/rules/{rule_id}` (right `moments.settings.write`) with the settings and `revision` changes one (409 `version_conflict` on a stale one). Audited. They fire when their emitters appear (tested on fixture events now); a later step may redefine its own rules.

| Rule | On | Shows (template, class, regions, TTL) | Ends early on (`source_ended`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `match_called` | `tournament.match.called` | `match_called`, `tournament`, hero → rail, 300 s | `tournament.match.started` of the match |
| `standings` | `tournament.leaderboard.changed` | `leaderboard`, `tournament`, hero → rail, 120 s, bound to the leaderboard | — |
| `finals_result` | `tournament.match.finished` of a final (`session.is_final`) | `winner_card`, `finals`, hero, 120 s | — |
| `pizza` | `pizza.order.placed`, `pizza.order.arrived` | `pizza_placed`, `announcement`, venue rail → ticker (the food box's own place, #771), 300 s | `pizza.order.cancelled` |
| `hot_game` | `games.surge.started` | `hot_game`, `hot_game`, hero → rail, 120 s | `games.surge.ended` of the game |

## Scenes (design sheet 12; D5, D6, E7, L10, L14, O7)
A scene sets a zone's mood by itself, so nobody needs to direct at 03:00: a set of conditions (when) and a set of changes (then). Moments decides each zone's scene; Screen composes it ([screen.md](screen.md#motion-and-burn-in-drift)). A scene never starts or ends a moment; it only changes how the zone's screens look and what may play.

- **A scene** (`Scene`): `key`, `name`, `preset` (the preset it was copied from, or null), `zones` (empty: every zone), `when` (conditions; any one is enough, and an empty list always holds), `then` (changes), `hold_minutes` (0 to 240) and `blend_seconds` (0 to 600). An event has at most 20, in priority order.
- **Conditions** (D6): `time` (every day of the event from `from` to `to`, HH:MM in the event's time zone, past midnight when `to` is earlier: one line for a four-day event); `agenda` (an item of one of `kinds` runs on the zone, as Screen's day's phase reads items: public, scheduled, of the zone or of no zone, one without an end counting an hour and quiet hours without one never running; or, with `starts_within_minutes`, one starts within that many minutes); `checkins` (over `over` arrival check-ins in the last hour, from `notify.checkin.recorded`); `presence` (members online `over` and/or `under` a count, from `bot.presence.counted`; never while no count of the last 10 minutes is known); `games` (a game is surging, from `games.surge.started` until `.ended`); `crew` (the scene's crew button, holding `minutes` per press). Presence and games are tested on fixture events until their emitters land. The outside signal stays planned.
- **Changes** (`then`): `motion` (a level, or `calmer` or `livelier`: one step from the event's level, as Follow the day moves it; null keeps it), `layouts` (zone key to layout id), `stage_mood` (the stage's mood: `build_up`, `doors`, `peak`, `wind_down` or `quiet_hours`), `palette` (the theme's `primary` and `accent` while it holds, or null; the palette only, never a whole theme, L14), `sound` (on, off, or null to keep the zone's), `quiet` (quiet mode: the level at most `calm`), `hero_off` (the hero's features it switches off: `bracket`, `leaderboard`, `countdown`, `playlist`, `photo`, `hot_game`, `today_numbers`). The sound gates read `sound` and `quiet` (#113).
- **Which scene a zone is in** (E7), at every change of what the conditions read and every 10 seconds: the crew button pressed for the zone wins until its time ends; else the first scene of the list that applies to the zone and whose conditions hold. A scene stays on for its `hold_minutes` against any scene lower in the list, even when its own conditions drop for a moment; a scene higher in the list takes over at once. With none holding, the zone has no scene and Screen follows the day's phase.
- **The crew button** (U11, E7): `POST /api/moments/scenes/{scene_key}/take` (right `moments.scenes.take`, an `Idempotency-Key` as every crew action) with optional `zones` (default every zone), for a scene with a `crew` condition. One tap, no confirmation; the scene wins for the condition's `minutes`, until that time ends or the same button is pressed again. One press per event: another scene's press replaces it. On air shows it as "Peak now" for the Peak preset.
- **The presets** (O7): five built-ins, platform data an event copies on its first use (the first scene pass, at revision 0); a later platform version never changes an event's copy. In this order, without hold or blend, they are step 3's day's phase exactly, so an event that never opens Scenes looks as before; [moments/follow-the-day.json](moments/follow-the-day.json) holds the cases both modules' tests check, Moments that it sends these scenes, Screen that it composes them as the phase.

  | Preset | When | Then |
  |---|---|---|
  | `quiet` Quiet | a `quiet_hours` item runs | quiet mode; mood `quiet_hours` |
  | `wind_down` Wind down | a `quiet_hours` item starts within 60 minutes | `calmer`; mood `wind_down` |
  | `doors` Doors | a `doors` item runs | `calmer`; mood `doors` |
  | `peak` Peak | a `tournament` or `ceremony` item runs, or the crew button (30 minutes) | `livelier`; mood `peak` |
  | `daytime` Daytime | always | mood `build_up` |

- `GET /api/moments/scenes` (right `moments.read`) → `Scenes`: `scenes`, `revision`, `copied` (false until the first use copied the presets), `zones` (each zone's `scene_key`, `since`, `why` and `heartbeat_bpm` as last sent) and `crew` (the button pressed now: `scene_key`, `zones`, `until`; or null).
- `PUT /api/moments/scenes` (right `moments.settings.write`) with `scenes` and `revision` replaces the list; `POST /api/moments/scenes/reset` with `revision` puts this platform version's presets back ("Reset to default"). Each puts every zone in its scene at once; a stale `revision` is 409 `version_conflict`, a bad list 400 `invalid_request`. Audited.
- **`moments.scene.changed`** per zone, only when its scene or heartbeat changed: `zone_key`, `scene` (the scene's `key`, `name`, `motion`, `layout` (this zone's, or null), `stage_mood`, `palette`, `sound`, `quiet`, `hero_off`, `blend_seconds`; null while no scene holds), `since` (when the zone's scene began), `why` (the condition that held, "the crew pressed it", or the hold), `heartbeat_bpm` and `actor_user_id` (the person whose action changed it, or null).
- **The visual heartbeat** (L10, P5): `heartbeat_bpm`, 4 to 30 beats a minute: the mood's own (`quiet_hours` 4, `wind_down` 6, `build_up` 8, `doors` 10, `peak` 12), one more for every five arrival check-ins of the last hour and for every 60 members online. Screen draws it.
- **Clone with an event** (D22): on `tenants.tenant.cloned` (the tenants module checked the series and the caller), the source event's priorities, rules and scenes become the new event's, where it has none of its own (scenes it never saved count as none); never the history, a live moment or a zone's scene. A second delivery copies nothing again.
- **Pages:** **Scenes** (Studio → Moments → Scenes, and the Screen studio's Scenes tab, sheet 12): the band chart (what the time and agenda conditions alone will do on each day of the event, for one zone), the list in priority order (move up and down, edit, add from a preset, delete), one scene's conditions and changes, hold and blend, "Reset to default", and the crew buttons. **On air** shows each zone's scene, since when and why, and the crew buttons ("Peak now", one tap).

## Cues and sound
A **cue** is a picture plus an optional sound (step 4 plan, [The sound model](../docs/briefs/step-4-moments.md#the-sound-model-d16)); either half may play alone. A moment's start plays its cue: a rule's `cue`, the crew spotlight `announce.attention`, the emergency `emergency.start`, its "All clear" `announce.cleared`. A new photo (`screen.photo.added`) plays `photo.showcase`, sound only. A renewal or an edit of a moment never plays its cue again (E4, L13).

### The model
- **Six categories, fixed,** the rule axis: `urgent` (priority 70, level 100 %), `result` (50, 80 %), `attention` (40, 80 %), `something_new` (30, 80 %), `ended` (20, 80 %), `ambient` (10, 35 %). Each has a level and an on/off per event. Events add cues, never categories.
- **Cues, as data per event,** seeded from the 25 legacy cues with their names, categories, on/off and cooldowns (poll and cast seeded off: no emitter yet), and the storyboards' `story.doors`, `story.midnight` (#114), `story.meal`, `story.checkin`, `story.record`, `story.photo`, `story.draw` and `story.chime` (the preset catalogue, #602). Each has `enabled`, `level`, `cooldown_seconds`, `catch_up_seconds` (default 120) or `catch_up_before_start` (the tournament cues' legacy windows), `once_per_source` (the tournament, strip and pizza cues), one or more `sounds`, an optional `voice_line`, and its picture: `effects` and `hold_seconds`. An event adds cues of its own; a built-in can be set back to its seed.
- **The library:** any sound can go to any cue. The default pack is CC0 with a credits file (`modules/moments/static/sounds/CREDITS.md`), served without sign-in at `/api/moments/sounds/pack/<file>`. An event's own sounds are Screen's `audio` assets (D23): uploaded through Screen, then linked here, at most 1 MiB and 30 s, with a warning over 10 s.

### The picture
When a moment's cue has `effects`, Moments picks one at random, never the cue's last one (L7), and sends it on the moment's overrides as the additive Override field `effect`: `name` (`pop`, `tilt`, `flip`, `ripple`, `slide`, `glow`, `ring_burst`), `hold_seconds` (the cue's, 60 to 120, L8) and `fade_seconds` (10). Screen hands it to the claim, and the player draws it on the box from the claim's `starts_at`: the move once, then the frame and glow hold and fade ([screen.md](screen.md#cue-effects)). The picture plays whatever the sound gates say.

### How a sound is decided (mockup 13)
On the server, once per zone, the same for every screen of the zone; each zone's decisions run one at a time under its row lock. The gates, in order, each "no" a reason code in the log:
1. `master_off`, `muted` (the timed mute).
2. The zone's energy (Screen's `GET /internal/screen/zones/{zone_key}/sound`): `party` and `lively` play every category, `calm` urgent, attention and result (`calm`), a quiet scene (its `quiet`, or quiet hours while the zone has no scene) urgent only (`quiet`).
3. `category_off`, `cue_off`, `no_sound`, `cooldown` (the cue's on the zone); while an emergency runs on the zone only urgent (`emergency`).
4. `dedupe` (the same appearance again), `once_per_source` (7 days), `outside_window` (a cue with a window before the start, measured from the start of the moment's source, a run-of-show item's, else from the moment's own start).
5. `coalesced` (the same cue accepted on the zone in the last 2 s), the flood guard: `flood_source` (the same cue and source within `per_source_seconds`, 10; for a story's sound the source is its run, whatever the cue: at most one sound per 10 s per run, Storyboards) and `flood_minute` (`per_minute`, 3, per zone); `budget` (`hourly_budget`, 20, per zone). Urgent passes all three (`urgent_passes`) and still counts. The minute and hour counts are Valkey counters per zone (D15).
6. One at a time: ambient never waits (`busy`) and keeps 30 s between ambient sounds (`ambient_gap`); only urgent interrupts a playing sound (`interrupted`); any other waits in the zone's queue, highest category first, at most 4 s from its request (`waited`, else `stale`). While a story's video plays its own sound on the zone, every other non-urgent sound is held (`video`).

A sound that passes is sent to the zone's screens as `moments.sound.played` with `play_at` (now plus 400 ms) and `catch_up_until` (its cue's window); a voice line plays right after its sound and counts with it as one. The zone's sound screen, when Screen reports it offline or with its audio locked, makes the log line `missed` with `device_offline` or `audio_locked` (E12, E13); the sound stays sent, and the screen plays it late if it comes back within the window. A zone without a sound screen, or with its sound off (its own switch, or its scene's `sound`), is `suppressed` with `no_sound_screen`.

### The sound log
`GET /api/moments/sound/log` (right `moments.read`): every decision, newest first, with `zone_key`, `cue`, `sound`, `outcome` (`played`, `queued`, `suppressed`, `dropped`, `missed`), `reason` (the codes above, `free` for a sound that played at once, `sound_check`; for a story's step also `hidden` where its round is not shown and `story_cap` over the story's cap, Storyboards) and `detail`. A story's video logs its own sound as the cue `story.video`. Kept as the history is (D21).

### The sound desk's paths
- `GET /api/moments/sound` (`moments.read`): the settings, the categories, and per zone this minute's and this hour's count, its energy, what it lets through and its sound screen.
- `PUT /api/moments/sound/settings` (`moments.settings.write`): the master switch, the volume, `hourly_budget`, `per_minute`, `per_source_seconds` and the six categories, validated whole; `revision`.
- `POST /api/moments/sound/mute` (`moments.take`, a crew action with its key): `minutes` 1 to 240, or 0 to unmute.
- `POST /api/moments/sound/check` (`moments.take`, a crew action): a sound on zones now, past every gate but the master switch; it counts in no budget.
- `GET /api/moments/cues`, `PUT` and `DELETE /api/moments/cues/{cue}` (`moments.settings.write` to write): the cues.
- `GET /api/moments/sounds`, `POST /api/moments/sounds` with `asset_id`, `DELETE /api/moments/sounds/{sound_id}` (409 `sound_in_use` while a cue names it): the library.

## Announcements (#115)
One form makes an announcement for every channel: the screen card, the ticker line, the Discord post (the bot, step 6) and a push through Notify (step 4 plan, #115; mockups 05 and 06). Paths: [moments/announcements.openapi.yaml](moments/announcements.openapi.yaml), referenced from `moments.openapi.yaml`.

- **An announcement** (`Announcement`): `id`, `status` (`scheduled`, `live`, `ended`, `withdrawn`), `level` (`info` or `urgent`; an emergency is Emergency's, above), `full_screen`, the texts as written (`title_text`, `body_text`) and as filled (`title`, `body`; null until it is sent), `variables`, `item_id`, `channels` (`screen`, `ticker`, `discord`, `push`, each a boolean), `zones` (empty: every zone), `duration_seconds` (60 to 7200, default 300), `when` (`mode` `now`, `at` with `at`, or `item` with `minutes_before`, 0 to 1440), `fire_at` (when a scheduled one goes out, as now known), `sent_at`, `expires_at`, `updated_until`, `ended_at`, `end_reason` (`expired`, `withdrawn`, `item_cancelled`, `item_removed`, `ended_on_air`) and `why`, `actor` (who sent it), `moments` (the ids of its `card` and `line` moments, or null).
- **Who:** anyone with `moments.announce` sends, edits and withdraws announcements, at either level (U3). Reading them needs the same right.

### Levels, regions and the full screen (L2, L3)
- The **card** is a moment of class `announcement` with template `announcement` (`title`, `body`, `level`, `starts_at`, plus `updated` and `ticker_line`, [screen.md](screen.md#widgets)). Its chain follows its level: **info** `["rail", "ticker"]`, **urgent** `["rail", "hero", "ticker"]`. Info plays the cue `announce.attention`, urgent `announce.urgent` (its picture a ripple from the card's edge, its sound urgent: it interrupts and is pushed in quiet hours).
- **Full screen** (`full_screen: true`, either level): the chain starts with `full`, then the level's (`["full", "rail", "ticker"]` for info). It takes the full-screen region in the theme's look (the `announcement` template at level `info` or `urgent`, never the emergency's fixed red), ranked as an announcement, and where a higher claim holds a region of the zone it degrades along its level's chain (Screen's placement: `full` is free only when no higher claim holds a role).
- An announcement is a crew moment: never refused, it waits its whole life for a region (E3: two senders at once both run; the second takes the next free region of its chain, or waits).

### Channels (D13, L4)
- **Screen card** (`channels.screen`, default on): the card above.
- **Ticker line** (`channels.ticker`): a second moment of class `announcement`, template `ticker` (`text` is the title and the body joined by " · ", `until`, `source` `moments`, `urgent`, `updated`), regions `["ticker"]`, no cue: a claim in the `ticker` role owned by Moments, with the card's end; Screen's manual ticker ([0018](../docs/decisions/0018-ticker-manual.md)) is untouched. The card starts a millisecond after its line, so on a zone where the card itself degrades to the ticker the card wins the role (Screen's tie-break: the later start), and the card then carries the line in `ticker_line` and draws **both lines** there (L4 B); the line waits on that zone.
- **Discord** (`channels.discord`): carried in the events for the bot (step 6), which posts, edits and marks withdrawn posts (D12). Nothing else in step 4.
- **Push** (`channels.push`): Notify's `announcements` topic, owned by `moments`, turns `moments.announcement.sent` into a notification when `data.channels.push` is true, urgent from `data.urgent` ([notify.md](notify.md#topics-and-sources), D20). Moments registers it at start: `PUT /internal/notify/topic-types/announcements` with the registration there.

### Templates and placeholders (D11, E17)
- A text may hold placeholders: `{item.title}`, `{item.starts_at}` (HH:MM in the event's time zone), `{item.starts_in}` ("15 minutes", "1 h 5 min", "now"; from the moment it is sent), `{item.location}` (empty when the item has none), `{event.name}`, and **free variables** the organiser defines: any `{name}` of `a–z 0–9 _` (a letter first, at most 32), filled from `variables` in the composer before sending. An `item.` placeholder needs `item_id`, an item of the run-of-show (the agenda Moments keeps, [Scenes](#scenes-design-sheet-12-d5-d6-e7-l10-l14-o7)).
- **Validation** (400 `invalid_request`, naming it): an unknown placeholder, a free variable without a value, an `item.` placeholder without an item or with an unknown one, a `{` or `}` that opens or closes no placeholder. A placeholder never shows raw.
- **Limits:** the filled title at most 60 characters and the body at most 140 (E17), checked when it is composed; the card fits its text to its box (the template's auto-fit). A scheduled one is filled again when it goes out: a text the new `{item.starts_in}` makes longer is cut to the limit with "…".
- **Templates** per event (`AnnouncementTemplate`: `key`, `name`, `title`, `body`, `level`, `builtin`, `variables`, the free variables its texts name, `revision`): four built-ins (`starting_soon`, `food`, `lost_found`, `doors_close`), platform data, plus the event's own. `GET /api/moments/announcement-templates` (`moments.announce`); `PUT` and `DELETE /api/moments/announcement-templates/{key}` (`moments.settings.write`; a built-in's key is 400; a stale `revision` 409 `version_conflict`). Audited. Clone with an event copies the event's own (D22).

### Sending, scheduling, editing, withdrawing (E3, E15, E16, L13)
- `POST /api/moments/announcements/preview` (`moments.announce`) with an `AnnouncementInput` answers what would go out, saving nothing (U11): the filled `title` and `body`, the `regions` of the card, the ticker `line`, the `push` (`title`, `body`, `urgent`) and `discord` (`text`) as their channels would carry them (null when off), and the `zones`. Validation as on send.
- `POST /api/moments/announcements` (`moments.announce`, a crew action with its `Idempotency-Key`) with an `AnnouncementInput`: **now** starts its moments and emits `moments.announcement.sent` (201, `live`); **at a time** (not in the past, at most 14 days ahead) or **with a run-of-show item** (`minutes_before` its start) keeps it `scheduled` (201) until `fire_at`. An item's delay moves `fire_at` with it (the agenda Moments keeps, read again on every `agenda.*` event). An announcement with an item (sent with it, or using it for placeholders only) is withdrawn when the item is cancelled or removed, before or after it went out, with `end_reason` `item_cancelled` or `item_removed` and `why` naming the item when it is `public`, and only "its run-of-show item" otherwise, because the event is public (E15). The tick sends what is due, also after a restart.
- `PATCH /api/moments/announcements/{id}` (`moments.announce`, a crew action) with `title`, `body` and `variables`: a live one changes in place everywhere, card and line, whatever shape each zone gave it (E16), with `updated: true` for 60 s (the "Updated" chip) and no new sound: the moments' `.changed`, and `moments.announcement.changed`. A scheduled one only keeps the new texts. An ended or withdrawn one is 409 `not_running`.
- `POST /api/moments/announcements/{id}/withdraw` (`moments.announce`, a crew action): ends its moments everywhere at once, silently (`cleared`, no cue), and emits `moments.announcement.withdrawn`; a scheduled one never goes out. An ended or withdrawn one is 409 `not_running`.
- It ends by itself at `expires_at` (`expired`, no event). A crew member who ends its card everywhere on On air withdraws it (`ended_on_air`).
- `GET /api/moments/announcements` (`moments.announce`): the live and scheduled ones, then the last 20 that ended or were withdrawn.

### Pages (U5, U11)
The composer is **Run the event → Announcements** (`/moments/announce`, a menu item for `moments.announce`), reached too from the phone's quick actions (On air's "Announce", and Quick add's "Announce something instead") and from "Announce this" on a run-of-show item (`/moments/announce?item=<item id>&template=starting_soon`). One form: a template, the title and body with their variables, the level and full screen, the channels, the zones and when. "Preview" shows each channel as it will go out and the card in the real player (Screen's zone preview, as Priorities); "Send" (or "Schedule") sends it. Beside it the live and scheduled announcements, each with Edit and Withdraw (one tap), and the event's templates ("Save as template" for `moments.settings.write`).

## Storyboards (#114)
Sequences for the peaks as data an organiser edits without code (the [storyboards plan](../docs/briefs/step-4-moments/storyboards.md#plan)): one or more triggers, conditions, a run mode, one round of steps with at most one inner repeat, the rounds, the ends, an optional finale and which rounds sound. Paths: [moments/storyboards.openapi.yaml](moments/storyboards.openapi.yaml), referenced from `moments.openapi.yaml`; a story's shape: [moments/storyboard.schema.json](moments/storyboard.schema.json).

### A story
- **`Storyboard`:** `key`, `name`, `preset` (or null), `enabled`, `triggers`, `conditions`, `run_mode`, `full_screen`, `round`, `rounds`, `end`, `finale`, `sound`. An event has at most 40.
- **Triggers** (any one starts it, SD2, SD4), each with the trigger time **T** its rounds count from:
  - `item` (`kinds` or one `item_id`, `anchor` `start` or `end`): a public, scheduled run-of-show item; T is that moment of the item and follows its delays; a cancel or a removal ends the run silently (SE3, E15). With no kind and no item it never fires until the organiser picks one (a preset's "pick an item"); a take still asks for the item.
  - `domain` (`type` from the fixed list in the schema, `final_only`): T is when the event arrives. A `pizza.order.done` with `reason` `timer` starts nothing: the scheduler closed an order nobody marked, so nothing happened in the hall to show (D3). A run keys on its source (`match:<id>`, `session:<id>`, `order:<id>`, `game:<id>`, `photo:<id>`).
  - `clock`: `once` (a date-time; with none set, `at` null, it never fires), `daily` (HH:MM in the event's time zone), `event_start`, `event_end`, or `every` n minutes (15–240) within a window `from`–`until` on the event's days, each fire a run of its own aligned to the window's start. T is the clock time.
  - `take`: a person (below). A take of a story with an `item` trigger asks for the item, and T is that item's time (SU3).
  - `scene` (`scene_key`, `enters` or `leaves`): a zone's scene changes; the story plays on that zone. T is the change.
  - `milestone` (`count` `checkins`, `online` or `photos`; `at` a value, or null for a new peak): T is the crossing.
- **The on-the-dot marker:** one step of a round may be marked; for `item` and `clock` triggers the round starts early by the length of the steps before it, so the marked step starts exactly at its round's time (Midnight's sting at 00:00:00, its countdown from 23:59:50). For the other kinds it has no effect.
- **Conditions** (all must hold when a trigger fires; a fire that fails one writes a history row `not_fired` with the reason, once per source): `zones` (empty: every zone) or `item_zone`, `scenes` and `not_scenes`, `energy_min` and `energy_max`, `window` (HH:MM–HH:MM), `days`, `once` (per `event`, `day` or `source`), `cooldown_minutes`, `quiet` (`calm`: in a quiet scene the story plays calm and silent, E11; `skip`: not on a quiet zone).
- **Run mode** (SD7): `per_source` (default: a second fire for the same source while its run is live is ignored), `restart` (the live run ends, a new one starts), `ignore` (one run at a time).
- **Placeholders** (D10, SD3): `{event.name}`, `{event.ends_at}`, `{now.time}`, `{now.day}` (the night of the event, past midnight counting as that night), `{item.title}`, `{item.starts_at}`, `{item.starts_in}`, `{item.ends_at}`, `{item.location}`, `{item.kind}`, `{item.game}`, `{next.title}` and `{next.starts_at}` (with `until_event: next_item_starts`), each domain type's public fields (`{session.name}`, `{match.label}`, `{winner.name}`, `{order.seq}`, …, in the schema's list), `{scene.name}`, `{milestone.value}`, and free variables a take asks for. An unknown placeholder fails validation; one that not every trigger fills needs a fallback text, `{item.location|the stage}`. Each round is filled when it starts; a `countdown` step with an empty `target_at` counts to T; a `qr` (`path`, `label`) in a step's content becomes the view-model's QR as Screen makes it.

### Timing (SL1, SL2)
- **A duration per step,** 1–60 s; a `sting` (1–5 s) is a transition, never the only step of a round.
- **The readable floor** of a step that shows text (stings and video apart), finales included: 3 s plus 1 s per 3 words, never under 8 s. **The minimum card time** of the round's message step: 15 s by default (6–60). **The busy venue factor** (×1, ×1.5, ×2) stretches text steps, never stings; a round it pushes over its limit drops its inner repeat, then the stretch stops at the limit, and no step goes under its ×1 length. Settings per event: `factor`, `min_card_seconds`, `airtime_percent`, `handover_seconds` (storyboard.schema.json `Settings`).
- **Limits:** a round is at most 120 s and 12 steps after its repeat is unrolled; a **long hold** story (`hold_seconds`, SL2) has one round whose last step is held so the round lasts up to 180 s. A video step's own length is outside these limits.
- **Repeats and rounds:** one inner repeat (`from`–`to`, 2–5 `times`); the rounds at `offsets_minutes` from T (−240 to +240) or `every` n minutes (2–120) from `from_minutes`. The gap between rounds is at least the round's length. Between rounds the region goes back to its rotation (SL5).

### Ends, the finale and the cap
- **Ends** (any one, at least one): `rounds` (1–20), `for_minutes` (up to 360), `until_clock` (HH:MM, the first after T), `until_event` (`item_starts`, `item_ends`, `next_item_starts`: the next public item on the zone; or a `domain` event of the run's source: a match started, an order closed, check-in closed), `crew` (Stop loop).
- **The finale** plays once on every planned end (a count, a time, a clock time, an event), never on the crew's Stop loop or an item's cancel; Skip to finale plays it at once. An end event that comes while a round plays ends that round early (`source_ended`). A later event of the run's source that takes it past its `domain` end (for `pizza.order.closed`: the order placed, arrived, done or cancelled) ends the run at once without its finale, and ends a finale on air (`source_ended`), so the screen never tells "Order closed" beside an order already called in (#771).
- **The 6 h cap** holds for `until_event` loops and for `every` loops whose only end is the crew's Stop loop: such a run ends at the latest 6 h after it started, history "ended: time limit", without a finale; the time a video step plays, with its pauses, does not count. A `clock every` story and a loop with `until_clock` end at their window; a `rounds` loop by its count, a `for_minutes` loop by its time.

### How a run plays
- **A run** is a row from its trigger to its end; the tick (every second) runs its rounds from the row alone, so after a restart it starts the round due now and skips the ones it missed (SE4). A round whose time ran out (its patience) is skipped and never played late.
- **A round is one moment** of class `storyboard` whose overrides carry `steps` (D9, SD1; `starts_at` the round's planned start, `expires_at` its end), with `source.ref` the run's source; Screen plays the steps from `starts_at` on the server clock, offline too. Each step carries its `transition` and, when its cue has a picture, `effect` (SD5); the Override's own `sound` and each step's `sound` stay null. A round with a video step is placed as back-to-back moments of the run: the steps before it, the video, the steps after it, each started at its planned time.
- **Its region held** (SE1): a round waits its patience (a quarter of the gap to the next round, at most 60 s; 120 s for a one-round story), then is skipped, "round 2 of 3 skipped: its region was held past its patience". **An emergency** (SE2): a round due while it holds every zone is skipped; the live round comes back if its time remains (E6). **Two stories** (SE7): both rank 65; the later start wins ties. A story's round is never refused for a held region: it waits, as people's moments do.
- **Quiet** (E11, SE6): in a quiet scene a story plays calm and silent (the sound gates' `quiet`), or with `quiet: skip` not on that zone; the finale too. A video step is skipped there; the round plays its other steps.
- **The phase hand-over** (SD9): a zone's new scene waits for a story's round live on the zone to end, at most `handover_seconds` (30 s, 0–120), then applies; never longer.
- **Airtime** (SO1): storyboards hold a zone's regions at most `airtime_percent` (40 %, 20–80) of any 10 minutes; a round that would pass it waits for the next minute, and is skipped ("airtime") if its time runs out. When a lower moment of the hero's chain has waited 60 s for a region, a story's next round waits until it had its place (the yield). A video round is never deferred.
- **One owner per happening** (WD1): a moment may carry a happening key (`type:source id`); while one live moment shows it, a second is refused with a history row ("not started: one owner per happening"). A story's domain round carries its trigger's; a built-in rule's moment carries its event's. A story's runs between rounds hold no key. **A preset and a rule** (SD6, WD2): a preset that is on turns its overlapping rule off for the event ([Presets and the crew](#presets-and-the-crew)).
- **History rows:** `round_started` ("round 2 of 3", "finale"), `round_skipped` (with why: its time ran out, its region was held, an emergency, not in quiet, airtime, one owner), `loop_ended` (with why: the item started, after its last round, its time is over, stopped by a person, cancelled, time limit), `not_fired` (with the condition). A test-fire's rows say `test-fire`.

### Sound in a story (SO2)
- **A cue per step,** decided per zone at the step's start, only where the round is placed and not hidden (else `suppressed`, `hidden`); only the first pass of an inner repeat sounds. **Which rounds:** `first` (default), `every`, `last` or `first_and_last` (the last two only when the last round is known: offsets or a count). The finale's cue plays when it plays.
- **The story cap:** at most 2 sounds a round and 6 a run per zone (`story_cap`). **The flood source of a story's sound is its run**, whatever the cue: at most one sound per 10 s per run (`flood_source`). Every story sound also counts in the minute's flood guard and the hourly budget per zone.
- A countdown is one sound of its whole length, never one per second.

### The video step (PV1)
- **A `video` step** names a Screen `video` or `youtube` asset ([screen.md](screen.md#content-and-scheduling)); the API keeps its `kind`, `url`, `title` and `duration_ms` (a file's length as Screen read it; a `youtube` asset's, which YouTube does not give, from the step's `duration_seconds`, up to 6 h). It plays full screen (regions `["full"]`, template `video`, ranked as a storyboard), to its own end, any length. Its content: `kind`, `url`, `title`, `started_at` (its start on the server clock), `paused_at` (or null), `muted_zones`.
- **Its own sound** plays on the zone's sound screen only, decided at its start as one story sound: the budget and the flood guard count it but never refuse it; it plays muted on a zone whose energy is `calm` or `quiet`, whose sound is off or muted (`muted_zones`). While it plays, every other non-urgent sound there is held (`video`); an urgent sound still interrupts it.
- **Preloaded:** while a story with an uploaded video step is on for the event, Moments sends `moments.prefetch.changed` with its file and the story's zones (on every save and reset), and Screen puts it in those zones' `prefetch`. A `youtube` asset must be public or unlisted (a private one fails its probe and the save is refused with that reason); it is not preloaded and needs the internet.
- **In sync:** every screen starts it at `started_at` on the server clock (an uploaded file within the 200 ms of the sound sync; YouTube best effort). A screen that cannot play it skips it, shows its rotation and reports why in its heartbeat's `errors`.
- **Interrupted:** an emergency or an urgent announcement on its zones pauses it (`paused_at`); when it ends, the video resumes where it stopped: Moments moves `started_at` and `expires_at` on by the pause, and the story's later rounds move by it too.
- **Quiet:** skipped in a quiet scene (the round plays its other steps), or the story is skipped with `quiet: skip`.

### Presets and the crew
- **The 24 presets,** platform data an event copies on first use (SD8, O7), in the plan's [catalogue](../docs/briefs/step-4-moments/storyboards.md#the-preset-catalogue-final) (1–5 by #114, 6–24 by #602): **every event**, on by default (Doors open, Starting soon, Match called, Champion, Midnight, Meal is served, Quiet hours soon, Good morning, Check-in open, Pizza order open, Closing time, Pizza last call, Pizza orders ready, Round start, Champion replay), and **some venues**, off by default (Arrival check-in, Hot game, Record crowd, Sponsor thanks, Prize draw, Group photo, Venue info, Stage break, Premiere). A later platform version never changes an event's copy: a preset the event has not copied yet is copied when it is turned on. Reset to default puts one preset (or every preset) back; clone with an event copies the stories and their settings. Their message cards draw as Screen's `title_card` widget (SL3).
- **Their particulars:** Pizza last call and Round start wait for `pizza.order.closing` and `tournament.round.started` ([events.md](events.md#pizza), provisional; steps 5 and 7 emit them) and stay idle until then. Prize draw and Group photo name no item, and Premiere's `clock once` no time, until the organiser sets one. Premiere (PV1) is a countdown, its video on the dot and an after-card; its `{item.*}` placeholders carry a fallback, since a `clock once` and a take without an item fill none, and a story whose video step names no asset yet may be saved only off (400 "choose the video before turning it on"). Champion replay is turned on and off with Champion in the picker; each can be turned off alone. Hot game's cooldown is the story's (30 min, any game).
- **A preset and a rule** (SD6, WD2): while Match called, Champion or Hot game is on, the `match_called`, `finals_result` or `hot_game` rule is off for the event; while Pizza orders ready is on, the `pizza` rule leaves `pizza.order.arrived` to it and ends its own moment of that order then, so the order has one voice (WD1); the placed order stays the rule's.
- **Who:** `moments.settings.write` edits stories and their settings; `moments.take` fires a story with a `take` trigger, test-fires one round on one zone (SU4), stops a loop and skips to its finale (SU1). Each is a crew action with its idempotency key, audited.
- **Pages:** Studio → Moments → **Storyboards** (`/moments/storyboards`): the preset picker (mockup 21: every event and some venues, each with what triggers it, its rounds and sound, its status in words such as "Next: Sat 19:00, Dinner", "Waits for a matching item in the run of show" or "Pick an item", and the rule it replaces; on or off, duplicate to edit as the event's own story, reset one or all to default, new story) and the builder (mockup 18): triggers as sentences, the conditions, the placeholders every trigger fills, the step strip with durations, floors, transitions and cues ("+ New cue" opens the sound desk with `story.<story>.<step>`), the repeat, the on-the-dot marker, the rounds' timeline, the meters (its airtime share, all stories' share per zone, sounds per run), the checks, a preview in the real player with a scrubber over one round and the whole loop with its gaps shortened, and test-fire. **On air** and the phone show Fire (with its item and free variables), Stop loop and Skip to finale.

## History (D21, mockup 09)
- Every decision is a row: a moment started, was refused, placed, hidden, queued (waiting), came back, changed or ended, a story's round started or was skipped, its loop ended, or it was not fired (Storyboards), with the zone (or none for the whole moment), the region, why (the rule and its event, or the person and the action), what it displaced, and how it ended. A renewal of the same content writes nothing.
- `GET /api/moments/history` (right `moments.read`) with filters `zone` (its rows and the whole-moment rows), `region`, `origin` (`rule`, `crew`, `emergency`, `module`) and `action`, newest first, `limit` (default 100, at most 500) and `before` (a row's `seq`, for the next page). `GET /api/moments/history.csv` answers the same rows, at most 10,000, as CSV.
- Kept for the event's life; deleted 30 days after the event is archived.

## Rights
U2 as amended by U3: `moments.read` (On air, History, Scenes, the settings read-only), `moments.announce` (announcements: send, edit, withdraw, read, either level), `moments.take` (the crew spotlight, ending and extending moments, the timed mute and the sound check), `moments.scenes.take` (the scenes' crew buttons, "Peak now"), `moments.emergency` (start and end an emergency), `moments.settings.write` (priorities, rules, scenes, cues, the sound settings and the library; announcement templates; storyboards). `moments.take` also fires, test-fires, stops and skips stories. Event admins hold all of them through their role.

**The "Moments crew" preset** (`moments_crew`): `moments.read`, `moments.announce`, `moments.take` and `moments.scenes.take`, a name for that list as `screen_crew` is ([screen.md](screen.md#rights)). `moments.emergency` is granted one by one to named crew.

## Audit
Through `POST /internal/tenants/audit` in the same request ([tenants.md](tenants.md#audit-log)): every crew action (spotlight, end, extend, a scene's crew button pressed or released), every emergency start and end (the venue-wide one in each event), every change of the priorities, the rules, the scenes (a reset too), the cues, the sound settings and the storyboards (a reset too), with the old and new values, each story fired, test-fired, stopped or skipped to its finale, the mute, the sound check, each sound linked to or taken out of the library, and every announcement sent or scheduled, edited and withdrawn, and each announcement template saved or deleted.

## Privacy
The history keeps the name and user id of whoever started or ended a moment. `GET /api/moments/me/export` answers the caller's history rows at this event; `DELETE /api/moments/me` removes their name and id from them, and from the storyboards' runs they fired ([README.md](README.md#privacy-export-and-delete)). `tenants.user.deleted` does the same in every event; `tenants.tenant.deleted` deletes the event's rows.

## Events (version 1)
Emitter `moments`, on the tenant's stream; listed in the catalog ([events.md](events.md#screen-overrides)). Audience event-wide, `public`.

| Type | When | `data` |
|---|---|---|
| `moments.override.started` | An override starts | `override` (Override), `actor_user_id` |

| `moments.override.ended` | It left zones or ended | `override_id`, `zones`, `reason`, `final`, `actor_user_id` |

| `moments.announcement.withdrawn` | A live announcement was withdrawn, by a person or by its item (E15) | `id`, `reason` (`withdrawn`, `item_cancelled`, `item_removed`, `ended_on_air`), `why`, `actor_user_id` |
