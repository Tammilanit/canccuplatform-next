# moments

The moments module serves [contracts/moments.md](../../contracts/moments.md): the engine part (issue #112), scenes (#582), cues and sound (#113), announcements (#115) and storyboards (#114). The engine is one server-side arbiter that ranks, times, queues and refuses every moment per zone, while Screen places them ([screen.md](../../contracts/screen.md#moments-drive-it-claims), step 4 plan D1). The step's plan is [docs/briefs/step-4-moments.md](../../docs/briefs/step-4-moments.md#plan).

## How it works

- **Tenancy:** each request's event comes from its `Host` (the kit's `installTenancy`). Every table is per tenant under RLS; work runs as `canccu_moments_app`. The global `moments_tenants` lists the events Moments serves (for the tick, the venue-wide emergency and retention) and `moments_outbox_tenants` the events with unsent events.
- **The arbiter** (`arbiter.ts`, pure; `engine.ts`, which sends through `reconcile.ts`): a moment is a row in `moments` from its request to its end. Its class ranks it by the event's priority order (`moments_priorities`, a zone may have its own). It is shown by overrides (`moment_overrides`, as last sent): one for the zones that follow the event's order, and one per zone whose own order ranks it differently (`<id>.<zone>`), which that zone keeps for the moment's life, because Screen never brings an override back to a zone it left. Every change is reconciled into `moments.override.started`, `.changed` and `.ended` (narrowing first, D3), stored in `moments_outbox` in the same transaction and sent after the commit (the kit's `Outbox`; a loop resends what a failed send left, every 2 s).
- **Legacy rules, regional:** TTL 120 s by default (1 to 7200); a request whose `dedupe_key` names a running moment renews it (E4: same content moves only `expires_at` and writes no history); a rule's or module's request is refused with a 30 s retry when every role of its chain is held by a higher rank on every zone it names, as Screen last reported (`blockedOn`); people's moments wait instead. A refused rule request is kept in `moments_rule_retries` (`rule-retries.ts`) and the tick asks again once its 30 s are over; until then the rule's own re-asks only refresh it, and it asks with the rule's settings of that moment and stops when the rule is off or a preset takes it over, when it starts, its source ends, or its TTL after the rule's last ask has passed. Patience counts only until a moment's first place on a zone.
- **Placement** (`placements.ts`): `screen.claims.changed` becomes `moment_placements` per override and zone, and each change a history row: placed, hidden, queued, or back after a higher moment ended, with what it displaced. Each zone keeps the newest report applied (`moments_claims_marks`: its stream entry, which orders reports where Screen's replicas' clocks do not, #657), so the replay of Screen's streams at boot (Zones, below) applies the reports Screen sent while Moments was down, and no report twice.
- **The tick** (`tick.ts`): every container, every second, visits `moments_tenants` and ends what is due from the stored times (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, the expiry pass only; ending, re-ranking and archiving wait for a lock): moments past `expires_at`, and waiting moments past their patience on a zone; then it asks refused rule requests again when due. Each story's triggers and each story run take their part of the tick in a savepoint of their own, so one that fails leaves the rest of that second to go on. A restart resumes from the rows alone (E2; `test/engine.test.ts` proves a takeover reverts on time across one). Hourly: idempotency keys after 24 h, and an archived event's history 30 days after archiving (D21).
- **Zones:** Moments keeps the event's zones in `moments_zones` from `screen.zone.saved` and `.removed` and from claim reports. A second subscription replays each screen stream from its start at every boot, changing only that set, so zones saved while Moments was down are known; a past removal never ends a moment running now (the live subscription does, E9).
- **Rules** (`rules.ts`): the five built-ins, on the catalog's outlines, tested on fixture events (`test/fixtures.ts`, held to `contracts/events.md` and `games.md`). Settings per event in `moments_rules` override the defaults. Each rule's `cue` plays when its moment starts (below).
- **Live data** (`sources.ts`): the latest leaderboard and each session with its matches (`moments_sources`, newest revision wins); a moment bound to one gets each change through `.changed`. A QR target becomes the view-model's `qr` as Screen makes it (`content.ts`).
- **Crew actions** (`routes.ts`, `http.ts`, `idempotency.ts`): the spotlight, end, extend and the emergency each run in one transaction with their audit entry (tenants' `POST /internal/tenants/audit`) and their idempotency key (`moments_idempotency`, E18). The venue-wide emergency (`module-routes.ts`) answers only on the superadmin hostname. Modules request moments on `POST /internal/moments/moments` (the kit's service identity).
- **Cues and sound** (`cues/`; #113, the plan's sound model D16): a moment's start plays its cue. The picture: `Cues.picture` picks one of the cue's effects, never its last (`moments_cues.last_effect`), and the moment's overrides carry it as `effect`. The sound: `cues/decide.ts` is the legacy sound engine, pure, deciding one zone (the gates of mockup 13 and their reason codes); `cues/service.ts` runs it per zone under the zone's row lock (`moments_sound_zones`: what plays until when, the 4 s queue, cooldowns, the last pick), with the minute and hour counts in Valkey (`cues/counters.ts`, D15), added only once the decision's transaction commits (`afterCommit` in `db.ts`, #657), the zone's energy and sound screen from Screen's `GET /internal/screen/zones/{key}/sound` (`cues/screen.ts`), and writes `moments_sound_log` and `moments.sound.played` (with `play_at`) in the same transaction. A cue's window before the start measures from the moment's run-of-show item (an announcement's), else from the moment's own start. A new photo, which every container gets, is decided by the one that claims its event in Valkey first. The tick pumps the queues. The cues are the 25 legacy seeds (`cues/catalog.ts`) overridden per event in `moments_cues`, plus the event's own; the library is the CC0 pack (`static/sounds/`, `CREDITS.md`, served at `/api/moments/sounds/pack/`) and the event's Screen `audio` assets linked in `moments_sounds`.
- **Scenes** (`scenes.ts`, `scene-eval.ts`, both pure; `scene-store.ts`, `scene-routes.ts`): the event's list in `moments_scenes`, copied from the five presets at the first scene pass (O7); each zone's scene as last sent in `moments_scene_zones`, the crew button in `moments_scene_crew`. A pass (`evaluateScenes`, serialised per event by an advisory lock) runs on every fact a condition reads (the agenda, read through `GET /internal/agenda/items` on each `agenda.*` event and once per container; arrival check-ins in `moments_checkins`; presence and surges in `moments_sources`), on a zone saved, a save, a reset, a press, and from the tick every 10 s, and sends `moments.scene.changed` for each zone whose scene or heartbeat changed. The presets reproduce Screen's "follow the day": `test/scenes.test.ts` checks them on [contracts/moments/follow-the-day.json](../../contracts/moments/follow-the-day.json), which Screen's tests check too. Clone copies the priorities, rules and scenes on `tenants.tenant.cloned`.
- **Announcements** (`announce/`; #115, mockups 05 and 06): a row in `moments_announcements` from its composing to its end; it shows through up to two crew moments, its card (class `announcement`, the level's chain, `full` first for full screen, the cue `announce.attention` or `announce.urgent`) and its ticker line (template `ticker`, regions `["ticker"]`, D13). The card starts a millisecond after its line, so where it degrades to the ticker it wins the role and carries the line in `ticker_line` (L4). Texts are validated and filled by `announce/text.ts` (the run-of-show placeholders from the agenda Moments keeps, `{event.name}`, free variables). `announce/follow.ts` is the tick's part: it sends what is due, moves item-attached ones with their item, withdraws on a cancelled or removed item (E15), drops the "Updated" chip after 60 s and ends an announcement with its card. Edits go through `setContent` (no cue), withdraws through `end` (`cleared`, silent). `moments.announcement.sent`, `.changed` and `.withdrawn` go through the outbox; `announce/topic.ts` registers Notify's `announcements` topic at start. The composer is Run the event → Announcements (`/moments/announce`, `announce/pages.ts`, `views.ts`, `list-views.ts`), whose preview draws the card in the real player (`static/moments.js`) as Priorities does.
- **Storyboards** (`story/`; #114, the [storyboards plan](../../docs/briefs/step-4-moments/storyboards.md#plan)): the event's stories in `moments_stories`, copied from the 24 presets of the catalogue on first use (`story/presets.ts`, built from `preset-steps.ts`: 1–5 in `presets-l5.ts`, the other "every event" ones in `presets-every.ts`, the "some venues" ones in `presets-venues.ts`; #602), with the timing settings and the event's facts the tick fills placeholders from. Pure: the model and its placeholders (`model.ts`), the checks (`schema.ts`, the contract's `storyboard.schema.json` through Ajv, first; then `check.ts`), the timing (`timing.ts`: floors, card time, the busy venue factor, the repeat, the long hold, the on-the-dot lead), the schedule (`schedule.ts`: round times, patience, skips, ends, clock fires), the builder's words and meters (`describe.ts`). On the tick (`triggers.ts`, then `runner.ts` and `round.ts`): `item`, `clock`, `scene` and `milestone` triggers fire runs (`fire.ts`: conditions, run mode, `not_fired` rows); each run is a row in `moments_story_runs` that plays its rounds as moments of class `storyboard` with `steps` (a video step as back-to-back moments), decides each step's cue per zone at its start within the story's cap and the run's flood source, pauses and resumes a video, skips what it must (held, emergency, quiet, airtime, its time ran out), and ends with the finale on a planned end. `domain` triggers and ends come with their events (`storyEvent`); a scene change waits for a live round within the hand-over (`handover.ts`). The API (`story/routes.ts`), the preset picker (`picker.ts` pure, `picker-view.ts`; mockup 21) and the builder (step 4 polish sheets 05 and 06: `story/pages.ts`, `builder.ts` with its parts `builder-overview.ts`, `builder-rounds.ts`, `builder-steps.ts`, `builder-side.ts` and `builder-fields.ts`, `form.ts`, and `static/story-builder.css` and `.js`, which load on the Storyboards tab only) and On air's Fire (one item picker and one Fire… chooser; `fire-words.ts` says when the story plays), Stop loop and Skip to finale; `words.ts` says the domain events in words. A preset that is on turns its built-in rule off (SD6; Pizza orders ready only the `pizza` rule's arrived order); a moment's `happening` key makes one owner per happening (WD1).
- **Archiving** (`tenants.status.changed` to `archived`) ends every moment, the emergency included (E10); writes then answer 409 `event_archived`. An archive sent while Moments was down is caught by the tick, which reads each event's status from the tenants module at most once a minute per container (`StatusTicker`).
- **Pages** (`pages.ts`, `views.ts`, `page-kit.ts`, `static/`): Studio → Moments, the Screen studio's Moments tab, at `/moments/` (Caddy strips the prefix): On air (`on-air-data.ts`, `static/on-air.css`; amendment 3, `docs/design/grids/02-on-air.svg`, #642: the crew actions as one band, the zones in `.canccu-grid` in Screen's order with at most four moments a card, then Storyboards and Scenes in `.canccu-band`; a claims report applies only on a zone Moments knows from Screen's zone events, so a removed zone never comes back), Spotlight, Priorities (with the built-in rules, and a preview in the real player through Screen's zone preview with `ranks`), Scenes (`scene-pages.ts`, `scene-views.ts`: the band chart, the list and its editor), History and Cues, the sound desk (#626, the step 4 polish sheets 01–04: four sub-pages, Cues, Library, Limits and categories and Log, under one master strip, and the cue editor; `cues/pages.ts` and `cues/editor-pages.ts`, `cues/views.ts` with a view per sub-page, `cues/cue-form.ts` and `cues/editor-side.ts`, `static/sound-desk*.css` and `static/sound-desk.js`; the library's upload goes to Screen's assets from the script, then links the asset). Zones show by the name Screen gives them (`zoneNames` in `page-kit.ts`, from Screen's `GET /internal/screen/zones`), their key while Screen does not answer. A page's form goes on to the API itself (`app.inject`, the same cookie and host), so it has the API's rights, idempotency and audit. The emergency starts on a 2-second hold (`static/moments.js`); without the script it asks once more.

## Environment

`CANCCU_DATABASE_URL`, `CANCCU_MIGRATE_DATABASE_URL` (the migration role), `CANCCU_TENANTS_URL`, `CANCCU_AGENDA_URL` (the agenda's internal read, for scenes and announcements), `CANCCU_NOTIFY_URL` (the `announcements` topic is registered there at start), `CANCCU_SCREEN_URL` (the sound gates and the library ask Screen), plus the kit's `CANCCU_MODULE`, `CANCCU_VERSION`, `CANCCU_VALKEY_URL`, `CANCCU_ADMIN_URL`, `CANCCU_SERVICE_KEY`. Optional: `CANCCU_SIGNIN_HOSTNAME`, `CANCCU_SUPERADMIN_HOSTNAME` (the venue-wide emergency answers only there), `CANCCU_IP_MODE=demo` (only with version `dev`).

Through Caddy: `/api/moments/health`, the contract's `/api/moments/…` paths, and the pages under `/moments/` (`/moments` redirects there). Not through Caddy: `/internal/moments/…`, and the internal paths, the API and health under the pages' prefix (404).

## Check

```sh
npm ci && npm run lint && npm run typecheck && npm test
```

The tests need a real Postgres, because RLS cannot be faked; without `CANCCU_DATABASE_URL` they are skipped. They drop and recreate the module's tables, so they refuse a database whose name does not end in `_test` ([docs/ci.md](../../docs/ci.md#modules)). `ci/stack-smoke.sh` checks the module on the running stack; `scripts/demo-stack-test` runs `ci/moment-latency.mjs`, which times crew spotlights from Moments to a paired screen's stream (p95 under 1 s, O2), and `ci/sound-sync.mjs`, which checks that two screens of a zone start a sound within 200 ms of each other and that only the zone's sound screen plays it.
